# Chapter 3 — Cell Studio

Reviewed the supplied OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e Chapter 3, PDF pages 101–144 (printed pages 85–128), including learning objectives, figures, key terms, summaries and review questions.

## Publication stages

1. **Chapter map and separate studio.** Create `/dist/chapter3/`, its own navigation and progress namespace, and a link from the existing atlas. Publish the six-module learning map.
2. **Learning atlas.** Add original explanatory lessons, clickable SVG diagrams, show/hide labels, textbook figure references, recall cards, mnemonics and a searchable glossary. Publish after content and layout checks.
3. **Practice studio.** Add six interactive process labs; module and chapter practice; multiple choice, typed recall and diagram location; balanced exams; scoring, answer explanations, missed-question retry and local mastery tracking. Validate and publish.

## Product decisions

- Chapter 3 has a separate question bank and exam. Existing Chapters 1–2 content and results remain separate.
- Desktop study screens fit the viewport. Long lesson, reference and review collections use pagination; smaller screens stack naturally.
- Original diagrams are stylized teaching models, labeled as such. Textbook figures are attributed and provided for comparison. No added decorative leader lines cross original reference figures.
- Explanations distinguish useful simplifications from precise biology. For example, CFTR is ATP-gated but chloride movement through it is passive; RNA polymerase reads the DNA template strand; mature red blood cells are exceptions to the generalized nucleated cell.
- Scores remain in this browser. Hinted responses are recorded as assisted. Full exams reveal answers after submission.
- Keyboard controls, visible focus, reduced motion and text explanations accompany the visuals.
