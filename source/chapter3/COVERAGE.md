## Clarifications to avoid misconceptions

CFTR gates with ATP but allows passive chloride movement down its electrochemical gradient. The template strand differs from the coding strand. DNA content doubles in S phase without doubling the centromere-based chromosome count. Exons retained after splicing can include untranslated regions. A unipotent stem cell can still self-renew; it is not simply any terminally differentiated cell. iPSCs can be reprogrammed from somatic cells; therapeutic usefulness is not automatic. Cytokinesis can overlap late mitosis. The labs explicitly state their simplified models and assumptions.
